Model assumptions and limitations

Descent uses the app's terminal-rate function at sea-level standard density. Catalog canopy diameter and Cd are used as supplied; they are not independently verified.

Packing uses the app's single-bay stacked-length model with 70% effective volume and radial screening. No obstructions are assumed within the usable space you enter. Shape, compression, extraction, retention and deployment are not validated.

These estimates are for exploring a main-chute choice, not a complete flight plan.
