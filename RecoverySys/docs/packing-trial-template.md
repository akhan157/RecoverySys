an empty-bay size reference are not a substitute for this observation.

## Repeatable packing states

Record each real packing state as a paired observation. A short, wide bundle
and a long, narrow bundle must not be combined into an unobserved short, narrow
state. Record conditions and method alongside each pair of dimensions.

## Whole-assembly observation

- Exact state used for each flexible item:
- How bags, protectors and hardware share space:
- Whether the bay closed, and the observed method/force (no inferred value):
- Contact, trapped fabric, sharp edges, snagging, access or routing concerns:
- Change after removing and repacking the same assembly:
- Unresolved questions and required independent review:

Static closure is an observation of that assembly and method. It does not
establish deployment clearance, ejection behavior, flight readiness or the fit
of a different packing state. Record any separately conducted deployment test
and its conditions as separate evidence; do not infer it from closure.
