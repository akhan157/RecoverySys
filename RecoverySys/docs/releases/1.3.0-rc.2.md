## What this candidate includes

- Compatibility findings for links and swivels using the existing snatch and
  opening-shock screens, derived synchronously from current inputs.
- Print provenance, result-currentness, evidence posture and unresolved
  mission-envelope checks in both printed artifacts; transient notifications
  stay on screen and out of print.
- OpenRocket import and transfer/print/evidence-intake currentness regressions,
  including saved plans, JSON and share links.
- Bounded Node 22/24/26 support, one-pass OpenRocket structure traversal,
  cross-file version identity checks and the fast-uri 3.1.9 patch.
- A 21-case review-only corpus and primary-source catalog research with staged
  differences. These remain evidence for review; they do not establish flight
  accuracy or new engineering values.
